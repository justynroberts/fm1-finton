Do not unplug the FM-1 while it writes. About a minute.

If anything goes wrong: replug and flash again (an unfinished update resumes). If the FM-1 will not start, hold OCT- while switching it on: the USB rescue mode, and this page still works from there. M-VAVE's updater puts the official firmware back.
